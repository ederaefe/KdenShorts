### PODCAST / INTERVIEW CONTENT CRITERIA:
Target Content:
- Surprising revelations, contrarian viewpoints, or provocative hot takes.
- High-energy conversational breakthroughs, sharp humorous punchlines, or sudden debate pivots.
- Gripping personal anecdotes with an immediate moral, business, or psychological takeaway.
- Relatable vulnerability or intense storytelling where the listener is hooked from the very first sentence.

Negative Constraints:
- NEVER clip podcast intros, sponsor reads, audio checks, or small talk greetings ("Thanks for having me").
- Cut out rambling mid-sentence pauses, heavy filler words, and unresolved banter.
- The clip must resolve completely before ending—do not cut off the punchline or the speaker's conclusion.
- Duration must be between 30 and 70 seconds.
