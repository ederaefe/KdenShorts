### SERMON / FAITH-BASED CONTENT CRITERIA:
Target Content:
- Standalone theological insights, paradigm shifts, or profound biblical reframings.
- Emotionally resonant, convicted preaching moments addressing universal human struggles (guilt, hope, purpose, suffering, faith).
- Counter-intuitive truth (e.g. challenging religious complacency or standard assumptions).
- Compelling personal testimonies or real-life illustrations with a clear moral payoff.

Negative Constraints:
- NEVER clip announcements, opening welcomes, offering appeals, or closing administrative remarks.
- NEVER clip scripture reading unless it is immediately followed by the core interpretive revelation.
- Avoid fragments that say "as I pointed out 10 minutes ago" or require earlier sermon context.
- Duration must be between 30 and 75 seconds. Must be a complete self-contained thought.
