### GENERAL SPEECH / KEYNOTE CONTENT CRITERIA:
Target Content:
- Powerful quotes, philosophical insights, key learning principles, and motivational takeaways.
- Compelling narratives that establish a problem, provide a transformative perspective, and deliver a clear resolution.
- Energetic speaking moments that provoke immediate curiosity or challenge the status quo.

Negative Constraints:
- Avoid greetings, logistical directions, or fragmented thoughts.
- Ensure the beginning starts on an active, intriguing hook without preamble.
- Duration must be between 30 and 75 seconds.
